Shade apparent temperature combines air temperature, humidity and wind speed (the same formula as the Berlin dashboard). Solar radiation is not included, so it is not a full heat-stress measure. Gemeinde values are area-weighted means of ICON-D2 grid cells (about 2.2 km). Kreis and Land values are means of their Gemeinden, weighted by area or by Zensus 2022 residents.

Residents: Zensus 2022 total population on 15 May 2022. Most unincorporated areas (gemeindefreie Gebiete) have no census record; they are shown with a forecast but without residents.
